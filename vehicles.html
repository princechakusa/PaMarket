Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find cars for sale in Zimbabwe?

PaMarket lists verified cars for sale from private sellers and dealers across all ten provinces of Zimbabwe, including Harare, Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru and Masvingo. Browse by make, price and location, then message the seller directly, free of charge.

Is it free to sell my car on PaMarket?

Yes. Posting a car for sale on PaMarket is completely free, with no listing fee and no commission taken when you sell.

What are the most popular used cars for sale in Zimbabwe?

The most searched used cars in Zimbabwe include the Honda Fit, Toyota Wish, Nissan Note, Mazda Demio, Nissan X-Trail and Ford Ranger, all commonly listed on PaMarket in USD and ZiG.

How do I stay safe when buying a car in Zimbabwe?

Always view and inspect the vehicle in person at a safe public location, verify the registration book and roadworthiness before paying, and avoid sending money before seeing the car. See PaMarket's Safety Guidelines for more.
