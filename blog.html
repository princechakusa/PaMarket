Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on posting, verification, pricing and staying safe.

Is it really free to post an ad?

payments

Yes. Posting on PaMarket has never cost anything and it stays that way — no listing fees, no commissions.

How do I get a Verified badge for my business?

verified

Set up a business storefront and submit your registration and ID documents for review. Once approved, your Verified badge shows on every listing.

Why do prices show in both USD and ZiG?

currency_exchange

So buyers and sellers can compare fairly in either currency. The ZiG figure updates against a live exchange rate.

A seller wants payment before I see the item — is that safe?

warning

No. Always inspect goods in person, in a public place, before paying. Report anything suspicious to our WhatsApp support desk.

Can I sell from my campus?

school

Yes — the Institutions hub lets students post and browse listings within their own campus community.

Can businesses advertise on PaMarket?

campaign

Yes — sponsored listings, category banners and campus placements are all available. See our Advertise page.
