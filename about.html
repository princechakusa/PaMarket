About PaMarket

A free marketplace built for Zimbabwe, by Zimbabweans.

PaMarket connects buyers, sellers, businesses and job seekers across all ten provinces. No listing fees, no commissions — just a safe place to trade.

Our Story

Why we built PaMarket

Buying and selling in Zimbabwe has always relied on word of mouth, scattered groups and the risk of dealing with strangers. We set out to bring it all into one trusted app, where listings go live in minutes and people deal directly without a middleman.

Today PaMarket is a full marketplace, a business platform for shops, and a hiring network — all completely free to use.
