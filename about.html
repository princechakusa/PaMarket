What PaMarket Actually Is

A Free Marketplace Built for How Zimbabweans Actually Trade

PaMarket is a website and mobile app where anyone in Zimbabwe can post a free ad, browse real listings, and message sellers directly — from a student selling textbooks in Harare to a registered business selling solar equipment in Bulawayo.

We don't charge listing fees, and we price everything in both USD and ZiG using a live exchange rate. Businesses can apply for a Verified badge by submitting real registration and identity documents for review — buyers can see at a glance which sellers have gone through that process.
