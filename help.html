Getting started
What is PaMarket?+
PaMarket is Zimbabwe's free online marketplace and classifieds app. It lets anyone in Zimbabwe buy, sell, rent property or vehicles, find jobs, and hire talent, all in one place, with no listing fees or commissions on sales.
How do I post an ad on PaMarket?+
Tap the "Post" button at the bottom of the screen. Choose your category, add clear photos, write a title and description, set your price, and select your location. Your listing goes live instantly — no waiting for review.
Is PaMarket free to use?+
Yes — completely free. Posting ads, browsing listings, sending messages, applying for jobs, and getting verified are all 100% free. No subscriptions, no commission on sales, no hidden charges.
What areas does PaMarket cover?+
PaMarket covers all ten provinces of Zimbabwe with hundreds of suburbs and towns: Harare, Bulawayo, Chitungwiza, Mutare, Gweru, Kwekwe, Kadoma, Masvingo, Hwange, Chinhoyi, Norton, Marondera, Ruwa, Beitbridge, Victoria Falls, Kariba, and many more.
Which app platforms are supported?+
PaMarket is available on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad, and as an Android app on Google Play. You can also use PaMarket in any web browser.
Does PaMarket have an AI help assistant?+
Yes. PaMarket Help is a free AI assistant inside the PaMarket app that answers questions about buying, selling, jobs, rentals and your account, in English, chiShona or isiNdebele. Open it from Account → Ask PaMarket Help, or Help & Support → Ask a question. It answers only from PaMarket's own help content, and if it is not sure it connects you with our support team. You need to be signed in to use it.
How do I get my account verified?+
Go to Account → Verify Identity. Enter your ID number, take a selfie, and upload a photo of your National ID or passport. Our team reviews your submission within 24 hours and your blue verified badge appears automatically when approved.
Managing listings
How long do my listings stay active?+
Listings stay active for 4 months. We remind you 3 days before an ad expires. To keep it live, go to Account → My Listings and tap Renew, which is free and gives it another 4 months. If an ad has already expired it shows as "Expired" in My Listings; tap Relist to put it back up for free.
Can I edit or delete a listing after posting?+
Yes. Go to Account → My Listings, tap the listing you want to change, then tap Edit to update photos, price, or description. To remove a listing, tap Delete. Deleted listings are permanently removed and cannot be recovered.
Why is my listing not showing up?+
Make sure your listing is set to "Active" in My Listings. Check that it was saved successfully — you should have received a confirmation. If your listing was removed by our moderation team, you will receive a notification with the reason. Contact support if you believe this was an error.
How do I know what price to ask?+
When you post an ad, PaMarket shows a price guide such as "Similar ads are priced $X–$Y", based on real asking prices of similar ads in your area over the last 6 months. Listing pages also show a "Typical price" so buyers can see whether a price is fair.
How do I get free boosts for my ads?+
Invite friends from Account → Invite Friends, Get Free Boosts. When a friend joins with your invite code and posts their first ad, you get a free 7-day boost to use on any of your active listings (up to 10 rewards every 30 days).
Buying & safety
How do payments work?+
PaMarket does not process or hold any payments. All payments are arranged directly between the buyer and seller — cash on collection, EcoCash, OneMoney, or bank transfer. Always inspect the item in person before paying, and never send money upfront for something you have not seen.
How do I contact a seller?+
Tap any listing, then tap "Send Message" to chat in-app, or tap "Call" or "WhatsApp" to contact the seller directly. All messages are stored in your Messages tab so you never lose a conversation.
Can I make an offer on a listing?+
Yes. Tap Offer on a listing, or Make offer inside a chat, and enter your price. The seller can Accept, Counter with a different price, or Decline, and you both see the offer in the conversation.
Where is the item located?+
Every listing shows its area on a map. Unless the seller has shared an exact pin, the map shows the approximate area of their town or suburb; the exact address is shared privately once you contact the seller.
How do I stay safe from scams?+
Never pay in advance without seeing the item. PaMarket warns you in chat if the other person asks for a deposit or EcoCash before you have seen the item, asks for an OTP or PIN, asks for courier or customs fees, or wants to move the conversation off PaMarket. Ads that reuse another account's photos are held for review, and verified sellers carry a blue badge. If a deal feels too good to be true, it probably is. Use the Report button to flag suspicious listings immediately.
Where is it safe to meet a buyer or seller?+
Listing pages show "Meet safely in" your city with suggested public meeting spots, such as well-known shopping malls, with map links. Always meet in a busy public place in daylight and inspect the item before you pay.
What does "Usually replies within an hour" mean?+
It shows how quickly a seller normally answers messages, based on their typical reply time over the last 90 days. The badge appears once a seller has replied to at least 3 conversations.
Can I rent a car on PaMarket?+
Yes. Open Rentals to browse vehicles from rental companies across Zimbabwe, with daily and weekly rates, minimum rental period, deposit and pick-up location. Choose your dates to see availability, then book or contact the rental company by chat, call or WhatsApp.
How do I report a listing or block a user?+
To report a listing: open the listing and tap the flag icon or scroll to the bottom and tap "Report". To block a user: tap their name on a listing or in Messages, then tap "Block User". Blocked users cannot see your listings or message you.
Jobs & account
How do I post a job as a company?+
Your company must be verified before you can post jobs on PaMarket. Go to Post → Jobs and follow the Company Verification steps. You will need your Certificate of Incorporation, Tax Clearance Certificate from ZIMRA, owner ID, and a photo of your premises. Verification takes up to 2 business days.
How do I track my job applications?+
Go to Account → My Applications. Each application shows its stage: Applied, Viewed, Shortlisted, Interview, Offer and Hired, and you get a notification when an employer moves you forward or invites you to an interview.
Can I download my CV as a PDF?+
Yes. Fill in your job profile under Account → My Job Profile / CV, then tap Download CV as PDF to create a clean, printable CV you can share or send to employers.
How do I delete my account?+
Go to Settings → Security → Delete Account. Type DELETE to confirm. Your account, listings, and messages are permanently removed within 30 days. This action cannot be undone — download any data you need before proceeding.
