How to Post

Ready in 4 steps

1

Choose your category

Select from 12 categories: Property, Vehicles, Electronics, Fashion, Furniture, Agriculture, Services, Kids, Pets, Sports, Business, and Other.

2

Write your listing

Add a clear title, honest description, price in USD, and choose your province and suburb. The more detail you add, the more enquiries you get.

3

Upload photos

Add up to 8 real photos of your item. Listings with photos get significantly more views. Use good lighting and show the actual condition.

4

Your listing is reviewed by our team and usually approved within 24 hours. You will be notified when it is live.

Tips for Success

Get more enquiries

Write a clear title

Include the brand, model, year, and key spec. "2018 Toyota Hilux Double Cab 4x4" outperforms "Hilux for sale" every time.

Set a fair price

Research what similar items sell for. Prices that are too high get ignored. Too low and buyers question the condition.

Use real photos

Take photos yourself in good daylight. Show any defects honestly. Buyers trust listings with real photos far more than stock images.

Choose the right province

Select the province and suburb where the item is located. Buyers filter by location so this determines who sees your listing.
