Vehicle Rental

Rent a car in Zimbabwe

Browse verified rental companies with daily, weekly and monthly rates. Cars, bakkies, SUVs and more.

PaMarket Vehicle Rental connects you directly with verified rental companies across Zimbabwe, offering cars, bakkies, SUVs and more on daily, weekly or monthly terms. Each listing shows real-time availability, transparent pricing including any deposit required, and the rental company's actual location, so you know exactly what to expect before you book. Rental companies are verified before their listings go live, and all bookings and payments are arranged directly with the company — PaMarket does not charge renters any booking fee. Browse by category and location to find the right vehicle for your trip, whether that is a short-term rental in Harare or a longer booking in Bulawayo.
