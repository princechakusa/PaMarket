FAQ
Frequently asked questions
What is PaMarket?+
PaMarket is Zimbabwe's free online marketplace and classifieds app. It lets anyone in Zimbabwe buy, sell, rent property or vehicles, find jobs, and hire talent — all in one place, with no listing fees or commissions on sales.
Is PaMarket free to use?+
Yes. Posting and browsing on PaMarket is always 100% free. There are no hidden fees, listing costs, or commissions on sales anywhere on the platform.
What areas does PaMarket cover?+
PaMarket covers all ten provinces of Zimbabwe, including Harare, Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru, Masvingo, Chinhoyi, Kadoma, Kwekwe and Victoria Falls, with hundreds of suburbs and towns.
How do I contact a seller?+
Open any listing and tap the WhatsApp or Chat button to message the seller directly. Contact details are never shared publicly until you choose to reach out.
