FAQ
Frequently asked questions
What is PaMarket?+
PaMarket is Zimbabwe's free online marketplace and classifieds app. It lets anyone in Zimbabwe buy, sell, rent property or vehicles, find jobs, and hire talent — all in one place, with no listing fees or commissions on sales.
Is PaMarket free to use?+
Yes. Posting and browsing on PaMarket is always 100% free. There are no hidden fees, listing costs, or commissions on sales anywhere on the platform.
What areas does PaMarket cover?+
PaMarket covers all ten provinces of Zimbabwe, including Harare, Bulawayo, Mutare, Gweru, Masvingo, Chinhoyi, Kadoma, Kwekwe and Victoria Falls, with hundreds of suburbs and towns.
How do I contact a seller?+
Open any listing and tap the WhatsApp or Chat button to message the seller directly. Contact details are never shared publicly until you choose to reach out.
How do I sell my car in Zimbabwe on PaMarket?+
Tap Post a Free Ad, choose Vehicles, add photos, the make, model, year and price, and pick your location. Your car listing is live in under two minutes and buyers message you directly — with no listing fee and no commission on the sale.
What is a verified storefront on PaMarket?+
A verified storefront is a free business profile with a blue verification badge, your own product catalog, a business inbox and lead tracking. It lets shops and small businesses in Zimbabwe list unlimited products and build buyer trust.
Is PaMarket a small business directory?+
Yes. Alongside classified listings, PaMarket is a directory of verified Zimbabwean businesses and shops. You can browse local storefronts, view their catalogs and message them directly across all ten provinces.
How is PaMarket different from Facebook Marketplace?+
PaMarket is built specifically for Zimbabwe: local categories, all ten provinces, USD and ZiG pricing, verified storefronts, a real jobs board, and private in-app chat so your phone number stays hidden until you choose to share it. Posting and selling are always free.
