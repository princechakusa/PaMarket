Where can I find phones and electronics for sale in Zimbabwe?

PaMarket lists phones and electronics from private sellers and local businesses in Zimbabwe. Browse the listings on this page, then message the seller directly in the app or on WhatsApp.

Is it free to sell phones and electronics on PaMarket?

How do I buy safely in Zimbabwe?

Power the device on and test the screen, battery, camera and charging port before paying. For phones, check the IMEI and that the device is not iCloud or Google locked. Ask for the original receipt or box where possible.
