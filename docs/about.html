About PaMarket

A marketplace built in Zimbabwe, for Zimbabweans, to buy, sell, and find work without paying a cent in commission.

Who we are

PaMarket is a Zimbabwean marketplace app. People list what they are selling, browse what others have posted, message each other directly, and arrange the deal in person. Employers post vacancies and job seekers apply, all in the same place.

It runs on your phone, works across every province, and does not take a cut of your sale.

Why we built it

Buying and selling in Zimbabwe often happens across scattered groups and noisy chats, where it is hard to search, hard to trust a seller, and easy to miss a good deal. We wanted one clean place where a listing has a photo, a price, a location, and a way to reach the person behind it.

We also wanted it to stay free. Posting an item costs nothing, and we never charge commission when you sell.

In-app chat, WhatsApp, or a call button on every listing.

🔵

Verified seller badges and one-tap reporting on anything suspicious.

How we keep it safe

  • Sellers can verify their identity with a government ID to earn a blue badge.
  • Listings are screened for banned content, and accounts caught scamming are removed.
  • Any listing or user can be reported from inside the app, and reports are reviewed.
  • Your data is encrypted in transit, and you can delete your account and all your data at any time.
