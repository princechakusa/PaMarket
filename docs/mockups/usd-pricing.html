How it works

$USD is the source of truth. Every price is stored, sorted, compared, and reported in USD — including subscriptions and boosts.
⇄ZiG auto-converts to USD on save. Sellers can still type in ZiG; the app converts it using the central rate and stores USD. ZiG is shown only as a reference estimate.
⚙One admin-set rate. You maintain a single USD→ZiG rate in Admin (app_settings); every device uses it, so conversions are consistent for everyone.
🛡Existing ZiG listings are safe. They display their USD equivalent immediately and are saved as USD the next time they’re edited — no risky bulk change.
