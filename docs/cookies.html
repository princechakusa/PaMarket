Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. What this covers

This policy explains the cookies and similar storage technologies used by the PaMarket website and app, what they do, and the choices you have. It sits alongside our Privacy Policy.

2. What are cookies and local storage

Cookies are small text files a website can place on your device. Local storage is a similar technology used by apps and modern websites to remember information on your device. Both let a service work properly and remember your preferences between visits.

3. What we use

  • Essential storage: The app keeps your sign-in session, your saved items, and your draft listings on your device so the app works and you stay signed in. Without this the app cannot function.
  • Preferences: Small settings such as your chosen language and whether you use dark mode are stored so the app remembers them next time.
  • Security: Our authentication provider sets a secure token so we can keep your session safe and signed in.

4. What we do not use

  • We do not use third-party advertising cookies that track you across other websites.
  • We do not sell or share this information with advertisers.
  • We do not build a profile of you for targeted ads from other companies.

5. Your choices

You can clear this storage at any time from your browser or phone settings, or by deleting the app. Doing so will sign you out and remove saved preferences on that device. If you delete your account, all of your data is removed as described in our account deletion page.

6. Changes

If we change how we use cookies or local storage, we will update this page and adjust the date above.
