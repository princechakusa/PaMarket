Help & FAQ

Quick answers to the questions we hear most. Still stuck? Reach us on the Contact page.

Is PaMarket free to use?

Yes. Creating an account and posting listings is free, and we never take a commission when you sell. You only ever deal directly with the other person.

How do I post an item?

Open the app, tap Post, add a photo, set your price, pick a category and your location, then publish. Your listing goes live straight away, with no approval wait.

How do I contact a seller?

Every listing has three ways to reach the seller: in-app chat, a WhatsApp link, and a call button. Use whichever suits you.

What does the blue verified badge mean?

It means the seller has confirmed their identity with a government ID. It is a sign you are dealing with a real, checked person. You can request verification in the app under your profile.

How do I stay safe when buying or selling?

Meet in a public place during the day, inspect the item before paying, and avoid sending money in advance to someone you have not met. If anything feels wrong, walk away and report the listing.

How do I report a scam or a bad listing?

Tap the flag icon on any listing or profile in the app to report it. Every report is reviewed, and accounts caught scamming are removed. You can also report by email from our Contact page.

How do I post a job or apply for one?

Choose the Jobs category to browse vacancies and apply with your details and CV. Employers can post a vacancy and manage applicants from inside the app. It is free for both sides.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Account, then Settings, then Delete Account, and confirm. This permanently removes your profile, listings, messages, and personal data. Full steps are on our delete account page.
