Does it cost anything to post an ad?

No. Posting on PaMarket is completely free, and always will be.

How does the Verified badge work?

A business submits real registration and ID documents for our team to review. Once approved, a Verified badge shows on their listings and storefront.

A seller is asking me to pay before I see the item — is that normal?

No. Never send money before inspecting goods in person, in a public place. Report the listing to us on WhatsApp immediately.

How do I report a listing or user?

Use the report option on the listing or profile in the app, or message our WhatsApp support desk with the link.
