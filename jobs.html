PaMarket lists live job vacancies from real employers across Zimbabwe, covering all ten provinces from Harare and Bulawayo to smaller towns like Chinhoyi and Kwekwe. Every vacancy is reviewed by our team before it goes live, typically within 24 hours, to keep fake or scam listings off the platform. Employers can post a vacancy for free, with no charge to advertise a role or contact candidates who apply. Job seekers browse and apply at no cost too — charging a candidate any fee to apply, interview, or be placed is strictly against PaMarket's rules and will result in the listing being removed. Whether you are hiring for a single role or looking for your next job, PaMarket Hire Talent connects you directly, without a recruitment agency in between.
