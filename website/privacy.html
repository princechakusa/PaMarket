Privacy Policy

How PaMarket collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: June 2026

PaMarket is committed to protecting your privacy. This Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the rights you have over it. By using PaMarket you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who We Are

PaMarket Zimbabwe operates the PaMarket website (pamarket.co.zw), Android and iOS mobile applications, and related services. We are the data controller for personal information collected through these services.

Contact: chakusaprince@gmail.com | WhatsApp: +971 58 977 2645

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us directly:

  • Account registration details: name, email address, phone number, and password
  • Profile information: profile photo and bio (optional)
  • Listing content: title, description, photos, price, location, and category when you post an ad
  • Business information: business name, registration details, product catalogue, and business category for Shop operators
  • Job and CV information: work history, skills, education, certifications, and availability when using Hire Talent
  • Messages: content of conversations between users on our platform
  • Support requests, feedback, and contact form submissions
  • Identity verification documents (national ID, passport) when you opt in to verified status

Information collected automatically when you use PaMarket:

  • Log data: IP address, browser type and version, pages visited, time and duration, and referring URLs
  • Device information: device type, model, operating system, and unique device identifiers
  • Location data: province and city you select when posting a listing, or approximate location from IP address; precise GPS location only if you grant explicit permission
  • Usage data: listings you view, searches you perform, and features you interact with
  • Push notification tokens: when you enable push notifications on your device
  • App version and crash reports to help us identify and fix bugs

Information from third parties:

  • Google Sign-In: if you register or log in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google, subject to Google's own privacy policy

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Create, manage, and secure your account
  • Display your listings to other users and power the marketplace search
  • Enable direct messaging between buyers, sellers, and businesses
  • Deliver push notifications for new messages, listing updates, and offers (if you have enabled them)
  • Operate the Hire Talent section and surface relevant candidates to employers
  • Verify your identity to issue a Verified badge and increase trust on the platform
  • Send transactional communications: account confirmations, listing approvals, and security alerts
  • Send marketing communications about PaMarket features and promotions, where you have opted in
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, spam, scams, and abuse
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines
  • Analyse usage patterns in aggregated, anonymised form to improve our platform
  • Comply with legal obligations under Zimbabwean law and court orders

4. Data Storage and Processors

PaMarket uses Supabase (supabase.com) as our primary database and authentication infrastructure. Data is stored on servers located in the European Union (AWS eu-west-1 region). Supabase is bound by a data processing agreement with us and implements strong security standards including encryption at rest and in transit.

Other third-party service providers we use include:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): delivery of push notifications
  • Apple Push Notification Service (APNS): delivery of push notifications on iOS
  • Google Fonts: loading web fonts on the website (IP address shared with Google)

All processors are contractually bound to handle your data only as instructed by us and to maintain appropriate security standards.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • With other users: Your listing details (title, description, photos, price, general location, and seller name) are visible to all users. Your phone number is only shared if you choose to display it on a listing. Business inbox messages are seen by the business account owner.
  • With service providers: As described in Section 4, we share data with processors only to deliver our services.
  • For legal compliance: We may disclose information to law enforcement, courts, or regulators where required by Zimbabwean law or a valid court order.
  • For safety: We may share information to protect the rights, property, or safety of PaMarket, our users, or third parties, including reporting illegal listings to the Zimbabwe Republic Police.
  • Business transfer: If PaMarket is sold or merged, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction, with notice provided to users.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. Specifically:

  • Account data is retained until you delete your account, plus up to 30 days to allow for recovery
  • Listing data is retained for the life of the listing and up to 12 months after expiry for dispute resolution purposes
  • Message data is retained for up to 12 months and then automatically purged
  • Identity verification documents are deleted within 30 days of verification completion
  • Log data is retained for up to 90 days for security monitoring purposes

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 8). We will process deletion within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher (HTTPS)
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text; Supabase uses bcrypt hashing
  • Database access is restricted to authorised systems and personnel only
  • Row-level security policies on our database prevent users from accessing each other's private data
  • Push notification tokens are stored securely and associated only with your account
  • We regularly review our security configuration and respond promptly to incidents

No method of internet transmission is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password and to enable Google Sign-In where possible for added account security.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

The PaMarket website uses browser cookies and localStorage to:

  • Keep you signed in between sessions (authentication token stored in localStorage)
  • Remember your recently viewed listings and search preferences
  • Store offline-capable app data for use when your connection is limited
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity

We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking cookies. You can clear localStorage data through your browser settings, though this will sign you out of PaMarket.

10. Children's Privacy

PaMarket is not intended for persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has registered on PaMarket, please contact us immediately at chakusaprince@gmail.com and we will take steps to remove the account.

11. Third-Party Links

Listings on PaMarket may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of every website you visit.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or applicable law. When we make significant changes, we will notify you by in-app notification or email at least 14 days before the change takes effect. Your continued use of PaMarket after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
