Community Guidelines

How to use PaMarket safely, fairly, and with respect for every Zimbabwean on the platform.

Last updated: June 2026

PaMarket is built on trust. These guidelines exist to keep our marketplace safe, honest, and welcoming for buyers, sellers, businesses, and job seekers across all ten provinces of Zimbabwe. Breaking these guidelines can result in listing removal, account suspension, or a permanent ban.

1. Honest Listings

Every listing on PaMarket must honestly represent the item or service being offered.

What to do

Describe the item accurately, including any faults or wear. Use real photos of the actual item. State a firm, honest price. Update the listing if the item is sold or the price changes.

What not to do

Do not copy stock photos from the internet and claim they are your item. Do not hide defects or significant damage. Do not list items you do not own. Do not create duplicate listings for the same item to game the search ranking. Do not list a low price as bait and then switch to a higher price when a buyer contacts you.

2. Prohibited Items

Listings for prohibited items will be removed immediately and may result in your account being permanently banned and the matter reported to law enforcement.

The following are never permitted on PaMarket under any circumstances:

  • Illegal goods or services under Zimbabwean law
  • Firearms, ammunition, military weapons, or explosives without required government licences
  • Controlled drugs, narcotics, or prescription medicine (other than by licensed pharmacists)
  • Stolen goods, counterfeit items, or goods that infringe on trade marks or copyrights
  • Illegally imported vehicles or goods that have not cleared Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA)
  • Adult, pornographic, or sexually explicit content of any kind
  • Human trafficking, domestic workers offered without consent, or any form of exploitation
  • Wild animals, protected species, or poached wildlife products (ivory, rhino horn, etc.)
  • Pyramid schemes, Ponzi schemes, or fraudulent investment opportunities
  • Currency, bearer bonds, or unlicensed money transfer services
  • Items that promote racial, tribal, or religious hatred

3. Listing Quality Standards

High-quality listings help buyers find what they need and help sellers get responses. We expect:

  • Title: Clear and specific (e.g. "Samsung Galaxy A54 128GB - Harare" not just "Phone for sale")
  • Description: Include condition, age, key specs, reason for selling, and location details
  • Photos: At least one clear, well-lit photo of the actual item. Multiple angles are better. No watermarks from other platforms. No screenshots of other listings.
  • Price: State a clear USD price. Write "Negotiable" only if you genuinely are open to offers.
  • Category: Post in the most relevant category. Electronics go under Electronics, not Services.
  • Location: Select the correct province and town/suburb so local buyers can find you easily.

4. Respectful Communication

Every person on PaMarket deserves to be treated with respect, regardless of who they are or where they are from.

Behaviour that will not be tolerated

Harassment, threats, or intimidation of any kind. Racist, tribalist, sexist, or otherwise discriminatory language. Sending unsolicited promotional messages (spam). Using the messaging system to solicit personal information for purposes other than the transaction. Attempting to move the conversation off-platform to avoid accountability.

Good practice

Respond to genuine enquiries promptly. Be clear and honest about what you are selling and what price you will accept. If you change your mind, let the buyer know rather than ghosting. Keep communication on PaMarket so both parties are protected.

5. Business Shops

Registered Business Shops are held to a higher standard because they carry a Verified badge. Business accounts agree to:

  • Maintain accurate product listings and mark items out of stock promptly
  • Respond to buyer enquiries within a reasonable time (ideally within 24 hours)
  • Honour any commitment or quote made to a buyer through the platform
  • Not use the business account to post personal classifieds unrelated to their business
  • Provide genuine business contact information and be reachable

Businesses that repeatedly receive unresolved complaints, ignore buyers, or engage in misleading practices may have their verified status revoked.

6. Hire Talent

The Hire Talent section is for genuine employment opportunities and real candidates only.

  • Job listings must describe a real, existing role with honest compensation information
  • Employers may not post jobs that require an upfront payment or "registration fee" from candidates
  • Employers may not discriminate based on gender, race, tribe, religion, disability, or age (except where legally permitted minimum age requirements apply)
  • Candidate profiles must contain honest and accurate information about qualifications and experience
  • Fake job listings designed to collect personal information (phishing) are strictly prohibited

7. Scam and Fraud Prevention

Online fraud is a serious problem in Zimbabwe. PaMarket has built-in safety features, but users must also protect themselves.

Common scams to watch for

Advance fee fraud (pay a deposit to "secure" an item that does not exist). Fake property rental listings. Vehicle listings priced far below market value. "Overseas buyers" who want to send a cheque for more than the asking price. Sellers who insist on full payment before you view the item.

How to stay safe

Always view items in person before paying. Meet in a safe, public place such as a bank, shopping centre, or police station. Bring a friend for high-value transactions. Use EcoCash or bank transfer over cash. Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, it probably is.

Report any suspected scam using the Report button on the listing or message thread, or contact us directly at chakusaprince@gmail.com.

8. Reporting and Moderation

You can report any listing, user, or message that violates these guidelines using the Report button in the app. Our moderation team reviews reports and takes appropriate action, which may include:

  • Removing the offending listing
  • Warning the account holder
  • Suspending or permanently banning the account
  • Referring the matter to the Zimbabwe Republic Police or POTRAZ

We do not publicly disclose the outcome of specific moderation actions, but all reports are taken seriously. False or malicious reports (reporting a competitor in bad faith) are themselves a violation of these guidelines.

9. Enforcement

PaMarket reserves the right to remove any listing and suspend or ban any account that violates these guidelines, at our sole discretion. Decisions on enforcement are final, though you may appeal by contacting us with additional context. Repeat offenders and those who commit serious violations (fraud, illegal listings, harassment) will be permanently banned with no right of appeal.

10. Questions or Feedback

These guidelines will evolve as PaMarket grows. If you have a suggestion, concern, or need to report something urgent, contact us:

  • WhatsApp: +971 58 977 2645
  • In-app: Use the Report button on any listing, message, or user profile

Thank you for helping keep PaMarket safe and honest for every Zimbabwean.
