Zero Fraud Campus Framework

How PaMarket Campus Communities Work

Zimbabwean parents and students lose money each term to informal social media groups. PaMarket asks that trades stay on-campus, in person, and cash-on-inspection.

1

Browse Your Verified School

Only accredited institutions from our registry are listed, to prevent fraudulent or made-up schools.

2

Inspect First, Pay Later

Never send EcoCash, InnBucks, or cash deposits in advance. Meet in person and inspect the item before paying.

3

Meet in Safe, Public Places

Handovers should happen in busy, well-lit campus locations, such as admin buildings, libraries, or student union areas.
