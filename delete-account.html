Delete Your Account

PaMarket Zimbabwe · Account & data deletion

You can permanently delete your PaMarket account and all associated personal data at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the PaMarket app and sign in.
  2. Go to Account → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Permanently Delete Account.

Your account and data are removed immediately and you are signed out.

Request deletion by email

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address on your PaMarket account and we will delete it for you within 7 days:

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and personal details (name, phone, email, photo, bio)
  • All listings you posted
  • Messages you sent
  • Reviews you wrote
  • Saved items and saved searches
  • Job applications you submitted
  • Verification documents (ID and selfie images)
  • Your sign-in credentials
This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

What may be retained

For legal, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution purposes, we may retain a minimal record of transactions or reports for a limited period as required by law. This data is not used to identify or contact you and is deleted once no longer required.
